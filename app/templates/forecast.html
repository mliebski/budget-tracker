How the forecast works

Each future month = recurring items scheduled in that month + planned purchases dated in that month + your typical spending: the average of each category over the last {{ window }} full months, not counting recurring, planned or one-off entries. The current month combines what you’ve actually spent so far with the expected rest of the month. Overdue planned items are counted in the current month.
