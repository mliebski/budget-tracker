How budgets work

Set a monthly amount for everyday categories (groceries, fuel). For bills that come once or a few times a year (insurance, property tax), put the yearly total under Yearly and choose either spread evenly (1/12 each month) or the month it is due. A main category’s budget is the sum of its lines. The reference columns show last budget year’s actual and your recent monthly average ({{ window }} months) to help you pick numbers.
